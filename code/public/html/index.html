How it works

From a paid bill to a manager's decision.

Step 1

Add your team

Add your managers and employees. Each person signs in and sees the portal for their role.

Step 2

Submit a claim

Employees enter the amount, date, category and reason, and attach a photo or PDF of the receipt.

Step 3

Approve or reject

Managers check the receipt and decide. The reason is saved with the claim, so the employee knows why.
